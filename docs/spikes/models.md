## Verdict

GO. 100% procedural hard-surface models work in Godot 4.7.2: ~860-line GDScript ViewMeshBuilder + one ~300-line spatial shader give a cohesive stylized look (olive/cream/rescue-orange NAPC, plus NEC/DEF/HAN tank styles with clearly distinct silhouettes) with zero external assets. Each model is ONE ArrayMesh, ONE surface, sharing ONE ShaderMaterial; team colour and ALL animation (turret yaw, barrel recoil+elevation, wheels, track scroll, rotors, radar, infantry walk cycle, factory door, deploy spades, blinkers) are per-instance via 4 instance uniforms, no extra nodes. Forward+ auto-instances: 400 mixed units = 42 draw calls incl. 4 shadow cascades (14 without shadows). Silhouettes are readable at the RTS camera (55 deg pitch): 27 px/m at 45 m height, 20 px/m at 60 m, 1080p (tank hull ~95 px / ~71 px, squad legible at 6x crop). Mobile renderer runs unchanged; gl_compatibility works after two fixes but has hard limits (see findings). Recommended design: model space +X right, +Y up, -Z forward, metres (sim->view yaw = -sim_angle_rad - PI/2); recipes as an op list mapping 1:1 to builder calls (brush, set_part, push/pop, box, tapered_box, extrude, prism+ngon, lathe/cylinder/frustum/dome/wheel, track_belt, pocket_box, greebles, mark/mirror_x, tier, model_scale, build); bake/cache meshes and icons; keep instance-uniform path for Forward+/Mobile and the '#define MM' INSTANCE_CUSTOM shader variant for Compatibility.

## Key findings

1. VERTEX CONTRACT (verified): COLOR RGBA8 = paint rgb (sRGB) + team-mask alpha; UV = planar face metres (+random offset <=3 m) for panel lines, TRACK: (lateral m, arc-length m); UV2 = (trunnion_y, trunnion_z) for barrel elevation; CUSTOM0 ARRAY_CUSTOM_RGBA8_UNORM as PackedByteArray 4 B: x=(ao4<<4)|(wear<<3)|Mat[PAINT0 METAL1 GLASS2 RUBBER3 EMISSIVE4], y=Part kind, z=param 0..255, w UNUSED; CUSTOM1 ARRAY_CUSTOM_RGBA_HALF as PackedByteArray 8 B via encode_half (or RGBA_FLOAT as PackedFloat32Array 16 B): xyz=part pivot in MODEL space, w=extra (wheel radius / recoil m / door travel m). flags param of add_surface_from_arrays = (RGBA8_UNORM << ARRAY_FORMAT_CUSTOM0_SHIFT) | (RGBA_HALF << ARRAY_FORMAT_CUSTOM1_SHIFT); presence bits are derived from the arrays. Engine-reported stride 52 B/vertex (half) vs 60 B (float), 16-bit indices; half costs ~35 ns/vertex to encode, pivot error <=4 mm.

2. PART KINDS decoded in the vertex shader: 1 TURRET yaw about pivot, 2 BARREL (recoil along +Z, elevation about UV2 trunnion, then yaw), 3 WHEEL (angle=-roll/radius), 4 TRACK (fragment tread scroll from UV.y-roll; belt loop oriented so top run moves forward), 5 ROTOR, 6 TAIL_ROTOR, 7 RADAR/fan, 8-11 LEG_A/LEG_B/ARM_A/ARM_B (walk phase locked to roll distance so feet do not slide; param = per-soldier phase), 12 BODY_BOB, 13 DOOR (per-slat travel so slats stack under the lintel), 14 BLINK (emission only), 15 DEPLOY (spades). Instance uniforms via GeometryInstance3D.set_instance_shader_parameter(Color as vec4): u_team; u_anim=(time, move, roll_m, turret_yaw); u_aux=(recoil, deploy/door/elevation, spin rad/s, -); u_state=(damage, selected, fog_mode, hit_flash). ViewModelRig extends MeshInstance3D wraps them (push_anim/push_aux/push_state).

3. BUILDER: all primitives are closed convex solids; winding is auto-oriented against an outward reference normal (Godot front faces are CLOCKWISE, this removes a whole class of authoring bugs). Bevels are real geometry (inset faces + chamfer strips + corner tris, ~4x verts of a plain box) and flagged 'wear' so the shader chips paint only on edges. Boolean-free panel insets via pocket_box (grid subdivision), 2D-profile extrusion of convex side profiles, ngon lofts (turrets, boat hulls), lathe/wheel/track_belt (convex hull of circles), seeded greebles, mirror_x with pivot mirroring, per-primitive tier (LOD class), uniform model_scale applied at build(), conservative custom_aabb covering animated parts, rest_aabb stored as mesh meta for camera/icon framing. Face-level attribute blocks keep GDScript fast (~0.7 us/vertex). Transform3D * PackedVector3Array exists natively.

4. SHADER (unit.gdshader): PBR per material class (paint/metal/glass/rubber/emissive), panel-seam lines with screen-space AA that fade with distance, edge chipping from baked wear flag + noise, height-based dirt, damage soot + embers, thin fresnel selection rim, hit flash, emissive lights with blink, quality uniform (0 = no procedural noise, -14% at 4K), and a FOG-OF-WAR HOOK: global sampler2D fow_tex + vec4 fow_rect registered with RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_add BEFORE materials compile, sampled once per unit in the vertex stage; hidden units collapse to a point (no discard, keeps early-z), explored-but-hidden units dim. Verified end to end incl. runtime global_shader_parameter_set (shots/fog_final.png). Two compile variants from one source: default (instance uniforms) and '#define MM' (INSTANCE_CUSTOM) built with Shader.code = '#define MM 1\n' + source.

5. FORWARD+ AUTO-INSTANCING: 400 units sharing one material and 7 meshes drew in 42 calls with 4 shadow cascades (13-14 without); instance uniforms do NOT break batching. MultiMesh gave no gain (36/8 draws, GPU time within noise) and loses per-instance shadow-cascade culling; its only win is script cost. Workload is fill/post-bound, not draw- or triangle-bound: at 4K, post-FX (SSAO+glow) and MSAA4x are the two big costs.

6. MESH LOD WORKS on procedural meshes: add_surface_from_arrays(..., lods={key: PackedInt32Array}) sharing one vertex buffer. Keys are world-space 'edge length' errors in metres compared to ~1 px: switch distance ~ key*H/(2*tan(fov/2)) (1080p, 40 deg: key 0.02 = ~35 m; measured sweeps 0.01/0.02/0.04 switch between 12-25 m / 25-50 m / 50-100 m). Dropping bevel strips from the shared buffer would open cracks, so LOD1/2 index sets reference duplicated plain faces and half-segment lathes (+35% vertices): tank 4750 -> 2650 -> 2448 tris, factory 2450 -> 1258 -> 982, total primitives in a 400-unit frame 2.43M -> 1.41M (-42%). Hint: my first sweep went the wrong direction (bigger key = later switch).

7. PITFALL 1: RGBA_HALF custom arrays must be a PackedByteArray of half bits (RS check 'p_arrays[ai].get_type() != PACKED_BYTE_ARRAY' -> 'Invalid array format for surface'); only *_FLOAT formats take PackedFloat32Array; RGBA8 formats take PackedByteArray. PITFALL 2: a runtime error inside a '-s' SceneTree _init leaves the process idling forever; always process_frame.connect(quit) and wrap runs in timeout.

8. PITFALL 3 (gl_compatibility): the 4th component (.w) of an RGBA8_UNORM CUSTOM attribute is NOT delivered (reads as 1.0), which turned 'wear' on everywhere = digital-camo paint (compat_units.png). Never use .w; packing into x/y/z fixed it (compat_v4.png). Integer bit ops, UV precision and the value noise were tested and ruled out. PITFALL 4: Compat instance uniforms cap at ~256 instances ('Too many instances using shader instance variables ... Maximum items supported by this hardware is: 4096'; 16 slots reserved per instance regardless of declared uniforms; 145 of 400 instances failed). Compat also has NO auto-instancing (801 draws, render CPU ~9 ms, 16.8 ms/frame at 1080p) and much darker ambient/ground (needs its own lighting calibration). Use the MM/INSTANCE_CUSTOM variant there. For >4k instances in Forward+/Mobile raise rendering/limits/global_shader_variables/buffer_size (inferred, untested; 800 instances worked).

9. PITFALL 5: Mobile renderer renders identically (only SSAO unavailable, one warning) but does not auto-instance (801 draws): risk is CPU on weak machines, GPU was fine. PITFALL 6: RenderingServer.viewport_get_measured_render_time_gpu returns 0.0 on Metal in 4.7.2 (CPU time works); RENDERING_INFO_BUFFER_MEM_USED does not move on mesh creation (use RenderingServer.mesh_surface_get_format_vertex/normal_tangent/attribute/index_stride); frame times below 8.33 ms are unresolvable (120 Hz present cap even with vsync disabled). PITFALL 7: team colour must contrast with faction paint (red team on DEF oxide-red hulls disappears) - reserve neutral team-plate styles or constrain team hues per faction. PITFALL 8 (tooling): zsh does not word-split unquoted variables (my first 4K matrix silently used defaults; rerun from a bash script); CLI arg --h collided with viewport height.

10. SubViewport BUILD ICONS work: transparent_bg=true + Environment.BG_CLEAR_COLOR + render_target_update_mode=UPDATE_ONCE gives corner alpha 0.0 in Forward+ (icons_final.png on a dark UI background). Cost: ~19 ms per icon including 2 frame waits, get_image()+ImageTexture.create_from_image at 256x192 = 0.8 ms; first icon 2.7 s on a cold Metal shader cache (47 ms warm). Recommend baking once at first run and caching PNGs in user://cache; frame from mesh meta 'rest_aabb' (bounding-sphere fit is loose for elongated models, use margin ~0.75).

11. LOOK ITERATION (>=3 rounds, all screenshots inspected): v1 lime 'toy' palette, hot sun, units seen from behind, blue team colour clipped flat -> v2 grade/framing, dark ground -> v3 solid dark track side plates (belts looked hollow/wireframe), uniform model_scale 1.25 for infantry (unreadable at RTS distance), thin selection rim (was washing the whole APC pale), olive/cream/rescue-orange palette, howitzer turret reshaped from 'bunker' to low casemate -> v4 panel-line fade thresholds (aliasing speckle on the cream factory roof), water specular blob, factions turned to face camera, factory door light strips moved in front of backdrop -> final set.
