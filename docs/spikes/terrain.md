Artifacts on disk under <repo>/prototypes/terrain/: project.godot, src/map (5 files), src/view (15 files), shaders/ (9), demo/demo_main.gd (demo + benchmark harness), shots/ (20 PNGs: final_t_* temperate, final_a_* arid dusk, evolution_1..5, renderer_*, decals_*, ui_fow_and_minimaps), results/ (8 raw benchmark logs).

## Verdict

GO. A zero-art, fully procedural terrain/water/atmosphere stack looks good from a 34-84 m RTS camera and is cheap in Godot 4.7.2. Scene: 192x192 cells (576 m), 295k-tri chunked terrain, 3.5k decor instances, water, 4-cascade sun, SSAO, glow, fog, colour grading and 150 units. It costs 4.4-4.7 ms/frame at 1920x1080 Forward+ on the M5 Max (Low preset 1.5 ms, Ultra 9 ms; at 2560x1440: 7.9 / 2.2 / 14.7 ms). It runs and looks right on Forward+, Mobile and gl_compatibility; Compatibility needs the workarounds listed under PITFALLS. Six visual iterations were done, all inspected with the image reader (evolution_1..5 plus final_*). Measurement caveat: the machine was shared (load average 16-19 on 18 cores), so deltas below about 0.3 ms are noise.

## Key findings

1. METHOD: Metal ignores --disable-vsync (every frame reads 8.33 ms at 120 Hz) and viewport GPU timestamps read 0, so timing uses RenderingServer.force_draw(false) back-to-back throughput, taking the min of p50 over 3-4 passes of 150 frames. macOS cannot open a 1920x1080 window on this panel, so a 16:9 window is used and the 3D pass runs at exactly 1920x1080 / 2560x1440 via Viewport.scaling_3d_scale (>1 = supersample); the final blit is cheap.

2. DESIGN terrain (ViewTerrain, MapTerrainData/MapTerrainGen/MapFbm/MapNoise): sim contract is integer-only: corner heights PackedInt32Array (1/32 m), per-cell types/flags/moisture, road polylines, salvage fields, 3-4 chamfer shore_dist; noise is lattice-cached integer value noise (Q8 in, Q16 out). The view bakes a 385^2 float grid (Catmull-Rom clamped to the middle pair, so no cliff ringing, plus view-only FastNoiseLite relief masked to ~0 on roads and start plateaus), then 64 ArrayMesh chunks of 24 cells (2401 verts, 4608 tris each). Arrays are position + normal (central differences of the GLOBAL grid, so no seams) + cavity in COLOR.r, with no UVs (shader uses world XZ) and one shared ShaderMaterial. WorkerThreadPool.add_group_task builds rows and chunks. raycast() is an adaptive march + bisection with no physics engine. Cliffs come from ~1.5 cell steep smoothsteps in the height layer plus slope-driven triplanar rock.

3. DESIGN terrain shader (terrain.gdshader): 6 layers from two 192^2 RGBA8 control textures (bilinear, domain-warped lookup, noise 'height' blending = organic borders); slope -> triplanar cracked rock; roads from a 768^2 raster (R = 255 - dist/6 m, G = signed lateral, BA = sin/cos of the along-road phase so lane dashes stay crisp under bilinear filtering); 385^2 R8 water-depth raster (exact waterline: wet sand, underwater tint, caustics); 1024^2 scorch overlay; two 256^2 RGBA8 noise/normal atlases from FastNoiseLite.get_seamless_image + Image.bump_map_to_normal_map (55-65 ms at boot); cavity AO; cloud shadows; fog of war. ~14 texture taps per pixel. A '#define TERRAIN_LOW' variant (Shader.code.replace, export-safe) saves ~0 on M5 but is kept for weak GPUs.

4. DESIGN water (ViewWater + water.gdshader): one 4-triangle plane (6 km, sea surrounds the island), blend_mix, NO screen/depth-texture reads (no copy pass). Body colour from the depth raster + shore-distance field, 3 scrolling normal layers, analytic sky reflection + sun glitter driven by global uniforms atm_sun_dir/atm_sun_color/atm_sky_horizon/atm_sky_zenith, pulsing shoreline foam band with fbm breakup. Disabling it changes frame time by less than the noise (<0.3 ms). Ship wakes should use foam quads, not a depth/screen copy.

5. DESIGN atmosphere (ViewAtmosphere + ViewMoodDef, two moods): ProceduralSkyMaterial with Sky.PROCESS_MODE_QUALITY + radiance 128; DirectionalLight3D; ambient from sky; ACES exposure 1.18; SSAO medium half-size; glow (softlight, HDR threshold 1.0-1.15); exponential fog + aerial perspective; a 1D colour-grade LUT (GradientTexture1D assigned to Environment.adjustment_color_correction: lift/gamma/gain split toning). Temperate day: sun 40 deg elevation / 222 deg azimuth, energy 1.55, ambient 0.78. Arid dusk: 17 deg / 250 deg, energy 2.1, blue-violet ambient (cool shadows against warm light), warm fog. Cloud shadows and fake sky reflections use the atm_* globals so all materials agree without probes or baked GI.

6. DESIGN cascaded shadows for a 34-84 m camera (ViewAtmosphere.fit_shadows(H, pitch, fov)): camera near = 0.72*H (ground begins ~1.0*H away, so no cascade texels are wasted), directional_shadow_max_distance = far_depth*1.09 + 12 (89 m at H=34, 114 m at H=54, 141 m at H=84), splits 0.16/0.38/0.68, 4 splits with blend, 4096 atlas, shadow_bias 0.04, normal_bias 1.4. Result is crisp and stable on terrain, trees and units.

7. DESIGN camera (ViewRtsCamera, reusable Node3D rig with a Camera3D child): WASD/arrows/edge-scroll pan with constant screen speed (pan_speed * distance), zoom-to-cursor via ground_pick_func, Q/E and MMB orbit, height 34-84 m with pitch 46 -> 61 deg, frame-rate-independent smoothing (1-exp(-k*dt)), map clamp, terrain-height follow via height_func, focus_on(), snap_to(), screen_to_ground(), static edge_scroll_dir() for tests. Edge scroll must be disabled in automation.

8. DESIGN fog of war (ViewFogOfWar + fog_of_war.gdshaderinc): the sim hands over ONE BYTE PER CELL (0 shroud, 1 fog, 2 visible) with no CPU conversion; Image.set_data + ImageTexture.update on two ping-pong R8 textures at 10 Hz; global uniforms fow_curr/fow_prev (sampler2D), fow_blend, fow_rect, fow_cells; shaders texelFetch the 4 nearest cells from both textures, bilinear on the decoded (explored, visible) pair, then a noise-fuzzed smoothstep; fow_blend is advanced per frame for a 100 ms cross-fade. Terrain, water, decor, unit and minimap shaders all include the same function (units: hide enemies / dim allies via fow_sample(NODE_POSITION_WORLD.xz) in vertex()). Verified visually on Forward+, Mobile and Compat. Upload cost is 4-12 us; the GDScript mark of 5 scouts (~1.4k cells) is 140-260 us; shader cost ~0.1-0.4 ms.

9. DESIGN decorations (ViewDecor, ViewMeshBuilder, ViewDecorMeshes): procedural low-poly broadleaf/conifer/rock/crystal/scrap = 420/164/118/300/180 tris. Vertex COLOR = baked albedo/AO + wind weight in alpha, UV.x = material class, per-instance phase/variation/emission in INSTANCE_CUSTOM. Wind sway is in the vertex shader (world-space gust direction, also runs in the shadow pass; offset converted to local space with transpose(mat3)/scale^2). Instances are bucketed into g x g MultiMeshInstance3D groups because a MultiMesh is culled as one AABB: g=1/2/4/6/8 gives 5.4/2.6/1.3/0.76/0.65 Mprims per frame. Use 4-6 per side (56-94 MultiMeshes); beyond 8 it becomes CPU-bound. Regroup costs 1-3 ms.

10. DESIGN damage marks: PREFER ViewScorchLayer (CPU Image.blend_rect of pre-made stamps into a 1024^2 RGBA8 overlay sampled by the terrain shader, bump from the alpha gradient): paint 2-4 us/mark, cost independent of mark count, 4 MB upload 0.65-1.2 ms (flush at <= 2 Hz), works on all renderers, but only affects terrain. ViewDecals (Decal ring buffer) is for special cases only. ViewBlobShadows (one MultiMesh of terrain-aligned quads) is the Low-preset shadow: 3.27 ms vs 4.68 ms with shadow maps; update costs 0.8-1.2 ms per 400 units in GDScript (cache normals to cut it).

11. DESIGN minimap (ViewMinimap): A) SubViewport + orthographic Camera3D (shares the World3D, own Environment, sees trees/shadows/water and gets fog for free, but re-rendering costs +3.5 ms at 1080p F+: 5.4 Mprims, 456 draws); B) CPU-baked 192^2 palette + hillshade image (21-42 ms GDScript once per map, 0 ms/frame, crisp stylised look). Both use minimap_fog.gdshader (canvas_item, same global fog state). Recommendation: hybrid = B as the default minimap + fog shader + unit dots via _draw; A only as an optional realistic view re-rendered at <= 1 Hz with a lean cull mask.

12. PITFALL 1: RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_get_list() logs 'should never be used outside the editor' at runtime; guard registration with a static var and register globals BEFORE creating materials. 'global uniform sampler2D' plus texelFetch works without hints in all three renderers, and also in canvas_item shaders.

13. PITFALL 2: 'instance uniform' values are NOT sRGB-converted, and Compatibility caps instance-uniform users at 4096 items (255 instances of a 2-uniform shader; instances 256-400 error out, units render black, thousands of log lines). Use ONE ShaderMaterial PER PLAYER COLOUR instead (also keeps Forward+ auto-instancing).

14. PITFALL 3: in Compatibility a MultiMesh with use_custom_data but WITHOUT use_colors feeds the custom data into COLOR (red trunks, glowing crowns: shots/renderer_compat_lake_multimesh_bug.png). Always enable both and write white colour (stride 20 floats: 12 transform + 4 colour + 4 custom). Also, MultiMesh.buffer must always equal instance_count*stride even when visible_instance_count is smaller (else 'different size' errors).

15. PITFALL 4: Decal nodes render on Forward+ and Mobile but NOT in Compatibility (verified visually), they also project onto units (set cull_mask), share max_clustered_elements (default 512) with lights/probes, and cost +0.3..1.4 ms at 64..1024 decals.

16. PITFALL 5: Mobile has no SSAO/SSIL (engine warns) and does not auto-instance (draws == objects). FXAA/SMAA are Forward+/Mobile only. TAA, FSR2 and MetalFX temporal were measured on Forward+ only; MetalFX temporal silently disables MSAA and requires scale 0.333-1.0; FSR/MetalFX warn and fall back when scale > 1. MetalFX spatial/temporal exist in 4.7 (Viewport.SCALING_3D_MODE_METALFX_*, macOS only, gate by capability). ProceduralSkyMaterial with PROCESS_MODE_AUTOMATIC selects INCREMENTAL (per-frame radiance work): set QUALITY (no measurable cost difference on M5).

17. PITFALL 6: baked vertex cavity/curvature computed from the DETAILED height grid produces 10 m 'camouflage' blotches; keep its gain <= 0.05 or derive from coarse heights. View-only relief noise must be masked wherever gameplay height matters (build areas, roads). Unit-tint albedo above ~0.5 overexposes under ACES (pastel); keep paint albedo 0.3-0.5.

18. PITFALL 7: Forward+ auto-instances identical mesh+material (80 draws for 384 objects; 150 tanks = 1 draw per pass) so per-unit MeshInstance3D nodes are viable; Mobile/Compat issue one draw per object (260-380). #include of .gdshaderinc plus global uniforms compile in all renderers; smoothstep(e0>=e1) is undefined; first frames of a run are slow (pipeline compile / GPU clock ramp), so warm up 3+ s before timing.

19. PITFALL 8: integer-only map generation is deterministic but slow in GDScript (360-410 ms at 192^2, ~0.7 s at 256^2). Lattice-cached noise (one hash per lattice point) and bounding-box early-outs were essential (naive per-sample hashing was projected at ~5 s). Rows are pure functions, so they can go to the thread pool if this ever matters. Terrain height baking scales badly with subdiv because of the bicubic path: 14/128/314/582 ms for subdiv 1/2/3/4 (74k/295k/664k/1.18M tris); subdiv 2 threaded is 160-230 ms vs 250-380 ms single-threaded.

20. RENDERERS: Forward+ = everything. Mobile = same look minus SSAO/SSIL, about 2x faster on this TBDR GPU, MSAA nearly free, decals OK. Compatibility = all our shaders (global uniforms, texelFetch, #include, canvas fog shader, SubViewport minimap, glow, cloud shadows) work after fixing pitfalls 2 and 3; no Decal rendering, no FXAA/SMAA/TAA; the look is slightly brighter/bluer (needs its own grade). The engine warns SSAO is available on Forward+ or Compatibility only (not visually verified in Compat).

21. DECISIONS for the architects: (1) keep the fog byte-grid contract, no CPU blur, two textures + GPU cross-fade, global uniforms for all shaders; (2) minimap = hybrid (CPU bake + fog shader + _draw dots), optional low-rate SubViewport render; (3) terrain LOD is unnecessary at this camera range (295k tris total), spend the budget on shadows/MSAA options; (4) persistent damage = scorch overlay, Decal nodes only where an effect must sit on buildings; (5) team colour via per-player materials, never instance uniforms; (6) decor in 4-6 groups per side; (7) Presets: Low = blob shadows, no SSAO/glow/MSAA, LOW shader; Med = 2 cascades 2048, SSAO low, FXAA; Full = MSAA 4x + SSAO medium half + 4 cascades 4096; Ultra adds SSIL, 8192 atlas, ultra PCF, MSAA 8x.

22. WITH MORE TIME (would look better): baked procedural PBR texture sets (tools/py) instead of noise-only ground; near-camera grass cards; cliff wall meshes or parallax; snow sparkle and wet-road puddles; planar/SSR reflection + refraction and Gerstner shore waves on water; decal atlas with normal/ORM; tree LOD/imposters; a 3D LUT per mood; a night mood; cached static shadow cascade; height fog / light shafts; better crystal and scrap-pile models; less blurry scorch stamps (higher-res or SDF stamps); faster blob-shadow update.

## Performance

Hardware: Apple M5 Max, Metal, Godot 4.7.2; 3D render 1920x1080 (or 2560x1440) via scaling_3d_scale; numbers are p50 ms of force_draw throughput, min over 3-4 passes, +-0.3 ms noise (shared machine).
FORWARD+ 1080p baseline 'full' (MSAA 4x, SSAO medium half-size, glow, fog, 4 cascades 4096, water, decor, 150 units): 4.68 ms (4.43 in another run), 80 draws, 1.32 Mprims.
