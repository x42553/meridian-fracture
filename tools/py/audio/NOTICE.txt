Meridian Fracture - licences of the audio generation toolchain (tools/py/audio)
================================================================================
Everything under game/assets/audio is generated by these scripts; nothing is recorded and nothing is copied from another
work.  game/assets/audio/provenance.json lists, per shipped OGG, the generator, model, voice, text, licence and tool versions
(schema fixed by qa.md 7.8; audited by validate_audio V-AUD-26); game/assets/audio/NOTICE.txt is the shipped credit line.

Music, sound effects, ambience, unit-response bleeps
  Procedural synthesis (numpy).  Licence of the output: procedural (project licence).  No third-party sample or model.

Announcer / EVA speech (game/assets/audio/vox/**)  -  text-to-speech, generated offline at build time
  Model      Kokoro-82M (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, v1.0), weights AND voices: Apache-2.0.
             File shipped nowhere; used from .cache/tts/kokoro-v1.0.int8.onnx (int8 ONNX export) + voices-v1.0.bin.
             The generated audio carries licence_model Apache-2.0 in provenance.json; credit line in NOTICE.txt of the assets.
  Runtime    kokoro-onnx 0.4.7 (MIT), onnxruntime 1.30.0 (MIT).  Build-time only.
  G2P        espeak-ng and phonemizer-fork (pulled in by kokoro-onnx): GPL-3.0-or-later.  BUILD-TIME TOOLS ONLY: they are never linked
             into the game, never shipped, and the synthesised audio is not a derivative work of them.
  Voices     af_heart (computer pack), am_michael (napc), bf_emma (nec), am_fenrir (olm), bm_george (def), af_bella (pd),
             af_nicole (han), am_puck (ae), bf_isabella (sap): the spike's placeholder mapping (docs/spec/audio.md 5.15); the choice
             needs ear review before release (audition sheet: .cache/audio/sheets/voice_choice.png after `check --sheets`).
  Effects    voice/chains.py: pure numpy computer / officer / radio chains (phase-vocoder pitch shift, magnitude-only robotisation,
             companded bit crush, compression, squelch, hiss).  ffmpeg is NOT required.
  Iteration  voice/tts_piper.py can render with Piper (`build_all.py voice --engine piper`) for fast turnaround.  piper-tts is
             GPL-3.0-or-later (own venv .cache/venv_piper, subprocess only, build time).  Only two voices are accepted:
             en_US-joe (CC0-1.0) and en_GB-cori (public domain, recorded as CC0-1.0).  Forbidden: hfc_male and ryan (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0),
             lessac (Blizzard-2013), and every voice whose licence has not been verified.  Piper output is never the shipped set.
  Forbidden  macOS `say` / any operating-system voice: its licence allows System Voices only for personal, non-commercial use.
             Nothing produced by an OS voice is ever written under game/.  (Runtime OS TTS for accessibility, spec 5.9 step 8, speaks
             the captions of announcer.json through DisplayServer.tts_speak and stores nothing.)

Encoding and analysis (build time only, not shipped)
  numpy (BSD-3-Clause), soundfile (BSD-3-Clause), libsndfile (LGPL-2.1), libvorbis (BSD-3-Clause), Pillow (HPND).

Caveats
  * No human listened to the output while it was built: quality is judged by loudness / true-peak / spectral / loop-seam / tempo /
    scale metrics and spectrogram sheets.  Voice choice, pronunciation (PRONUNCIATION in voice/lines.py) and music taste need an
    ear pass (spec 10.6 review gate).
  * If Kokoro is unavailable on a machine the committed OGGs stay valid; regeneration then fails with an explanatory message
    (bootstrap.py --voice).  A synthetic formant "computer voice" fallback was NOT needed: Kokoro works offline on macOS arm64.
